# Illustrated class spirits, v2

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, followed by a layout edit. Final project asset: `Skill/VFX/class-spirit-actors-v2.png` (1254×1254 RGBA). Four complete motifs, in a 2×2 grid: sword array, crescent lotus, thunder dragon, golden phoenix. The original PNG alpha is preserved. `js/11d-spell-imagery.js` caches complete quadrants, fits their visible bounds and deforms adjacent strips with shared edges; it does not swap full-pose paintings. Existing class formation artwork supplies skill 3. Teleport remains an outline tear.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D xianxia MMORPG spell actor atlas on genuinely transparent background. Create one square 2048x2048 PNG atlas with exactly four large isolated illustrated spell motifs, one per quadrant in an evenly aligned 2 by 2 grid. Each motif completely inside its quadrant with generous 12% transparent padding, absolutely no overlapping neighboring cells, no cropped tips. No panels, labels, letters, human characters, scenery, circles of background fog or opaque background. Fine polished hand-painted game art with crisp dark colored contour lines, jewel details, luminous but controlled highlights, strong silhouettes readable over pale stone floors. Upper left: a blue cyan and antique gold celestial sword formation, one magnificent broad central upright sword, six smaller complete swords fanning outward, floating ice crystal shards, tangible metal blades. Upper right: a mint jade and silver crescent moon spirit, three ornate curved lunar sickle blades enclosing a translucent jade lotus crystal, elegant flowing turquoise ribbons. Lower left: a complete vivid purple and indigo eastern thunder dragon coiling in a tall S, fierce clearly detailed head pointing right, four limbs with claws, horns and flowing mane, visible scales, warm gold dorsal spines, tapered complete tail; isolated dragon itself not a framed scene and no giant light cloud. Lower right: a complete golden ivory phoenix spirit flying with both wide wings clearly articulated and flowing long split tails, small hovering jade-gold paper talismans at its sides, a feather fan motif at the chest. Painterly depth and rich material detail with crisp readable edges; avoid photographic effects, abstract light streaks as main subject, excessive bloom, blank fog, text and watermark. All four motifs equally substantial, high quality game sprite artwork, true transparent alpha.

## Layout edit prompt

Edit this transparent game atlas. Keep exactly the same four beautifully detailed subjects, same palette, same quadrant order and style. Change only layout/spacing: scale each of the four entire motifs down to occupy no more than 74% of the width and 74% of the height of its respective quadrant, centered individually. Maintain an entirely empty transparent gutter on both sides of the exact horizontal and vertical centerlines. Every motif including all horns, whiskers, glow, blade tips, wings, tails must be fully inside its own quarter with 12% minimum transparent padding on all four sides. Absolutely no parts cross into another quarter, no overlap or cropping. Actual transparent background, no labels and no backdrop. Keep dragon, phoenix, blue sword array and jade crescent lotus sharply detailed. The central gutter is critical because each complete quadrant will be used as a separate sprite.
