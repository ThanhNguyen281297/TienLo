# pixelfx sprite sheets

The `.webp` sheets in this folder were rendered from **pixelfx** (https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx),
Copyright (c) 2026 Patch and Hugo Duprez, MIT License (see `LICENSE` in this folder).
Parts of pixelfx adapt Sprite Fusion's open-source pixel art VFX, Copyright (c) 2026 Hugo Duprez, MIT License.

Effects used: spawn/poof, impacts/hit-spark, impacts/crit-hit, holy/heal, arcane/blink-in,
spawn/respawn-beam, pickups/collect-burst, ui/level-up (text removed).
Frames were cropped and, for the two longest effects, halved to 15 fps. The Patch Pixel font is not used.
